Cinematic gothic fantasy with sculpted procedural forms and finely engraved painterly interface details. Twisting organic silhouettes surround angular ancient masonry and layered, articulated steel armor. The forest is deep petrol teal and moss green, the knight bright weathered silver with a wine-red cape, and danger a restrained ember red. Warm amber campfires and pale gold canopy shafts cut through cool atmospheric mist. Elevated isometric framing preserves clear silhouettes, navigable paths, and legible combat cues.
