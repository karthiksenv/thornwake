Painterly low-poly dark fantasy rendered with procedural faceted geometry, tapered organic silhouettes and fractured ancient stone. Environments use deep pine, muted sage and charcoal; the knight wears brighter silver-black plate with a green heraldic shield. Amber marks sanctuary and pale crimson marks hostile intent. Layered cool mist meets soft golden shafts of light for a melancholic, mystical woodland. Elevated third-person perspective keeps combat silhouettes distinct and nearby paths readable beneath a broken canopy.
